Assistant Professor

Maranus Huajie Chen

Trustworthy AI + Biosecurity

I am an assistant professor at the City University of Macau. My research focuses on trustworthy AI, including AIGC and model watermarking and AI attacks and defenses, and biosecurity, including watermarking biological foundation models and studying attacks and defenses in biological generative models. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Australia, under the supervision of Prof. Tianqing Zhu and Prof. Shui Yu. Previously, I earned my master’s degree at Universität Tübingen, Germany, where I collaborated with Prof. Nico Pfeifer, Prof. Oliver Kohlbacher, and Dr. Mete Akgün. I spent four memorable years at Sun Yat-sen University, where I earned my bachelor’s degree. I am always looking for motivated Ph.D., master’s, and bachelor’s students. If you are interested in working together, please feel free to email me.

Our Work "Osmosis Distillation: Model Hijacking with the Fewest Samples" has been accepted by IEEE TDSC! Our Work "CSC: Turning the Adversary’s Poison Against Itself" has been accepted by ACISP 2026!
