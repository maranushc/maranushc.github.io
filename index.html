Research program

Technology people can trust.

My research focuses on Trustworthy AI, Biosecurity, Cybersecurity & Privacy to develop accountable systems that remain safe and useful in the real world.

01

Trustworthy AI

Building transparent, reliable AI systems that are worthy of trust in high-impact settings.

02

Biosecurity

Examining how emerging technologies can be developed and deployed with biological risks in mind.

03

Cybersecurity & privacy

Advancing digital systems that safeguard security, privacy, and individual autonomy.
