Research program

From noisy measurements to biological insight.

Our group builds interpretable models that connect molecular variation to cell behavior, with a focus on methods that remain useful beyond the dataset they were trained on.

01

Predictive cell models

Learning compact representations of cell state that anticipate response to perturbation and treatment.

02

Gene regulation

Reconstructing dynamic regulatory programs from multimodal single-cell measurements.

03

Reliable computation

Creating transparent, reproducible tools for biological discovery across laboratories and populations.
